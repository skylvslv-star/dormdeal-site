Privacy policy

This policy explains how Dorm Deal (“we,” “us”) handles personal information in the DormDeal mobile app and on this website.

Effective and last updated: November 24, 2026
Contact: dormdeal.marketplace@gmail.com

The essentials. Dorm Deal uses account information, listings, messages, and related records to operate a campus marketplace. Seller applications include a home address. Supabase provides the backend, Stripe processes optional in-app payments, and Expo helps deliver notifications.

You can edit your profile and request account or data deletion. Deletion instructions.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile: email address, username, account identifier, password-based sign-in information handled by Supabase Authentication, optional profile photo, school affiliation, account role, and email-verification status.
  • Marketplace activity: listing titles, descriptions, categories, ISBNs, prices, condition, payment preferences, photos, saved listings, ISBN alerts, offers, and sold status.
  • Messages: message content, sender and recipient identifiers, conversation and listing references, timestamps, and read status.
  • Campus content: event and club information, posters, organizer details, contact information you choose to post, and related registrations or bookmarks when you use those features.
  • Neighborhood seller applications: your verified email, home street address, optional apartment or unit, city, state, ZIP code, neighborhood, selling plan, and application and review status. The owner uses this to review eligibility to sell near Biola.
  • Requests and support: school requests, school email domains, requester emails, feedback, and information you send us by email or otherwise provide for help.
  • Payments: for optional in-app charges, Stripe receives payment and billing information through its payment interface. Dorm Deal handles transaction references, amounts, status, account identifiers, and related entitlements or event records. Full card details are handled by Stripe rather than stored in Dorm Deal’s marketplace database. Payments between buyers and sellers for items take place outside Dorm Deal.
  • Technical information: notification push tokens and delivery records, authentication and session data, and service logs that can contain IP addresses, request times, device or app information, errors, and identifiers needed to run or protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to create and authenticate accounts; verify email and school access; review seller applications; display listings and campus content; support messages and offers; save listings and alerts; deliver service notifications and emails; process optional charges; answer requests; troubleshoot problems; and prevent misuse or unauthorized access.

Providing account information is necessary to use features that require sign-in. Photos, messages, seller applications, optional charges, and support requests involve additional information only when you use those features.

Who can see your information

  • Other users: your username, profile photo, and the listing or campus content you publish can be visible to users with access to that community. Contact details you include in a club, event, listing, or message are shared with its audience. Images available through public storage URLs can be accessed by anyone who has the link; do not upload sensitive documents.
  • Conversation participants: messages are stored on the backend and made available to participants. Do not treat chat as an end-to-end encrypted channel. Notification previews may display message text.
  • Dorm Deal administrators: authorized administrators can handle account, content, and support information as needed to run the service. Seller application email and home-address details are available to the applicant and the owner reviewing the application; the application address is not shown on listings or shared with buyers through that feature.
  • Service providers: the services below process information to provide hosting, storage, authentication, payments, messages, or notifications.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or secure the service. Information you share with other users can be copied or retained by them outside Dorm Deal.

Services that help operate Dorm Deal

  • Supabase: account authentication, database records, uploaded media storage, realtime messaging, and backend functions. Supabase privacy information.
  • Stripe: optional in-app payments such as extra listing capacity, event hosting, and donations where available. Payment requests include the amount and an account identifier. Stripe privacy policy.
  • Expo and phone notification services: push tokens, notification content, and delivery information are used to deliver alerts through Expo and platform services such as Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging. Expo update services may also receive device and update-request information. Expo privacy policy.
  • Google/Gmail: support correspondence and service emails, including seller-application notifications, are handled through our email service. Google privacy policy.
  • Open Library: when you scan a textbook barcode, the app can send its ISBN to Open Library to look up the title. That service also receives normal connection information such as an IP address. Internet Archive policies.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this public website and processes normal web-request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

External payment apps, stores, and websites you choose to visit apply their own policies. Providers may process information in countries outside your own; this policy does not promise that all information stays in a particular country.

Permissions and device storage

Camera access supports item photos and textbook barcode scanning. Photo-library access lets you choose images to upload. Notification permission lets the app send alerts. You can change these permissions in your phone settings; related features may then be limited.

The app stores sign-in sessions and feature preferences or status locally on your device. The app’s marketplace is based on school affiliation and information you provide; it does not require continuous GPS location tracking. A home address is requested when you apply as a neighborhood seller.

This public website has no account forms, advertising pixels, or added analytics scripts. Its links can open other services or your email app. The hosting provider still receives the request information described above.

Retention and account deletion

Account, content, and activity records are kept as needed to provide the service and address support, payment, security, or legal needs. Some content is subject to cleanup processes. We do not promise a fixed semester-based deletion date for all messages or an immediate purge of every copy.

You can edit your profile and manage listings or conversations using the app’s available controls. Removing a conversation from your view does not necessarily erase every underlying message or another participant’s copy.

To start account deletion: open Profile, tap the pencil icon to open Edit Profile, and hold “Delete Account” for five seconds. The current in-app flow deletes the profile and attempts to remove related marketplace records. It may not remove every stored record, including sign-in records, uploaded files, seller applications, and operational or payment records.

To request complete account and data deletion, or if you cannot sign in: email dormdeal.marketplace@gmail.com from your registered address. Specify that you want your account and associated data deleted. We may verify account ownership before acting. Some information may need to remain for legal, transaction, fraud-prevention, or security reasons; provider records, backups, and information copied by other users may have separate retention limits. See the support instructions.

Your choices and requests

Use Edit Profile to change your username or photo, manage the content you post, and use your device settings to control permissions and notification previews. Do not include private information in content visible to other users.

You can email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about its use. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including a copy of your information or the ability to object to or restrict certain processing. We will handle requests under applicable law and may need to verify your identity.

We use account access controls and backend authorization to limit access to information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Dorm Deal is designed for college communities and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when this policy changes and revise the date above. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide it.

For support or privacy requests, contact the Dorm Deal team at dormdeal.marketplace@gmail.com.
