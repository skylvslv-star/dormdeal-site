Dorm Deal support

A little help
goes a long way.

Questions about your account, a listing, or something that isn’t working? Get in touch with the Dorm Deal team.

Email the team

Let’s get it sorted.

dormdeal.marketplace@gmail.com

For a technical issue, please include:

  • Your device and app version
  • What you were trying to do
  • The error message or a screenshot with private details removed

Never send your password, sign-in codes, or full payment-card details.

I can’t sign in or verify my email.

Use the email address you registered with. Check your spam folder for verification mail. Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen if you need a reset. If a link fails, email support with the error you see.

Who can use Dorm Deal?

The current community focuses on Biola. Biola students use their school email. Non-students can browse and message after registering and verifying their email. Neighborhood sellers must apply and receive approval before listing. Access for other schools is currently paused.

How do I apply to sell near Biola?

Open “Neighborhood sellers” in the app. Verify your account email, then submit your home address and a description of what you want to sell. The owner reviews eligibility and location before approving selling access. Your application address is not displayed on listings.

How do payments work?

Buyers and sellers agree on payment for marketplace items directly, such as cash, Venmo, or Zelle. Dorm Deal does not process those item payments. Optional in-app charges for extra listing capacity, event hosting, and donations use Stripe where available.

For an in-app charge or billing issue, email support with the date, amount, and receipt or transaction reference. Do not send a full card number.

How do I manage notifications or photo access?

Use your phone’s system settings to change Dorm Deal’s notification, camera, and photo permissions. Notification previews can show message text on your lock screen, depending on your phone settings. Camera and photo access are used for the photos and barcode-scanning features you choose.

How do I report a listing or a safety concern?

Email support with the listing title, username, and a description of the issue. Include only information needed to investigate. Arrange exchanges in a public place and avoid sharing private home details in chat or listings.

How do I ask about my personal data?

Email support from your registered address to request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to verify that the account is yours. See our privacy policy for more information.

Account & data deletion

Ready to leave Dorm Deal?

  1. In the app, open your Profile and tap the pencil icon to open Edit Profile.
  2. Press and hold “Delete Account” for five seconds to start deletion of your profile and related marketplace records.
  3. For a complete account and data deletion request, or if you cannot sign in, email dormdeal.marketplace@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Dorm Deal account deletion.”

The current in-app flow may not remove every record, such as sign-in records, uploaded files, or seller-application details. Please contact us to request removal of those records too. We may need to verify your identity, and some records may need to be retained for legal, payment, or security reasons. Deletion can be permanent; save anything you need first.
